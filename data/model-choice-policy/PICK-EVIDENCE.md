# Benchmark candidates and provisional picks

The objective is the lowest complete workflow cost per accepted result at the required standard. AA's frontier narrows the candidate set. Its benchmark dollars do not measure that objective for our tasks. A dominated benchmark setting remains available when the owner permits it; a frontier change alone does not justify removing it or replacing a pick.

## What this release enforces

No configured default, candidate identity, effort or evidence basis changes in v0.2.10. The chart reads v5 facts; `model-policy-ops route` reads the versioned operating points and operational inputs, not the chart, benchmark performance files or recommender output. The recommender is advisory and cannot write the policy. Regression tests pin that boundary and preserve reasoned Luna max and Haiku max overrides.

`calibrate` and route-time outcome notes are descriptive and do not reorder candidates. Owner overlays, directives, availability and quota scheduling can select another permitted arm. These are explicit operational or owner choices, not proof that the chosen arm has the lowest measured cost per accepted result. Receipts record the overlay, overrides, authorizers and substitution reason. An unvalidated arm stays unvalidated even under an authorized scheduling exception.

Parent/checker work, repair and escalation linkage, consistent acceptance judgments and complete costs are not established by these records. Accepted closes therefore cannot establish a cheaper workflow or a causal model comparison.

This is a gap, not evidence that those picks are optimal. `review.rule-check` also has own closes, but its small typed-check study must not be described as benchmark-only or as complete real-task cost evidence. The GUI and Grok rows lack outcome evidence in this window.

## What does not silently change a pick

Changing an AA score, cost, chart frontier or advisory recommendation does not rewrite `expands_to` or `candidates`; no runtime path imports that frontier. A new default still needs a reviewed, versioned policy decision. The owner's overlay is a separate explicit policy input and is preserved by this refresh. The two max-effort maker choices remain usable even though neither is on the global v5 frontier.

## What is not enforced yet

The router does not automatically choose the empirical least-cost complete workflow: the required comparable observations are missing. This release adds no learned ranking, arbitrary minimum-sample threshold or fabricated USD conversion. A future cost-supported pick change must identify the accepted-result standard, model version and effort, full workflow boundary, missing costs, uncertainty and owner decision. Until then the defaults are provisional choices, not measured optima. Raw local records and identifiers are excluded from this public pack.
