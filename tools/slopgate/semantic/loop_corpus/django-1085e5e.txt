Fix RemoteUserMiddleware to not prefix HTTP_ for custom headers under ASGI

Under ASGI, RemoteUserMiddleware was incorrectly prefixing the HTTP_ string
when looking up custom header values in request.META. This change restores
the behavior prior to Django 5.2, where custom headers (e.g., HTTP_AUTHUSER)
are looked up directly without the additional prefix. The default REMOTE_USER
header behavior remains unchanged.

Refs #35883
