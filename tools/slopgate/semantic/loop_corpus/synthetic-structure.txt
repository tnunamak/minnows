# Connector runtime migration

## Background

The connector subsystem has existed since early 2025. It was originally designed for three connectors and has grown to twenty three. The original design assumed connectors were trusted. Over time we added retry logic, then rate limiting, then a cursor store. Each addition was reasonable on its own.

## Considerations

There are several factors to weigh. Memory isolation matters. Startup cost matters. Operational complexity matters. Debuggability matters.

## Details of the approach

Each connector will run in its own process. The supervisor will enforce limits. The limits will be configurable.

## Impact

The Amazon connector leaked a file descriptor on 2026-08-14 and took down eleven unrelated syncs for four hours.
