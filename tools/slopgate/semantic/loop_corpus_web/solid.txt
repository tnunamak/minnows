Solid: your data, your choice. Advancing the Web to empower people and communities.

Today's web often prioritises profits over people. Solid returns the web to its roots by giving everyone direct control over their own data. Your data lives in a Pod, a personal online datastore you control, and you decide which applications may read or write to it.

Solid is a set of open specifications built on existing W3C standards. Get a Solid Pod, or try the apps if you already have one.
